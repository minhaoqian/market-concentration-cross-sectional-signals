### Headline findings

- Baseline VW 12-2 momentum: **+0.0746%/month**, statistically imprecise.
- Excluding the Top-10 companies raises the VW spread by **+0.1099 percentage points/month** in point estimates.
- Decomposition: **+0.1114 pp/month direct-weight effect** and approximately **zero re-ranking effect**.
- A +10pp rise in aggregate Top-10 market share is associated with a **+24.48pp larger Q5-minus-Q1 mega-cap weight gap**; the relation remains positive after a linear time trend.
- Industry neutralisation leaves Rank IC essentially unchanged.
- Beta hedging reduces realised market beta from about **-0.38 to -0.16**, but does not reveal a statistically robust hidden momentum premium.
- External validation: correlation with Kenneth French Mom is **0.875 for VW** and **0.917 for EW**.

### Core conclusion

> **Market concentration appears more relevant to the portfolio implementation of value-weighted cross-sectional momentum than to the informational content of the momentum ranking itself.**

The strongest result is a mechanism result, not a causal return-prediction claim: concentration increases the mega-cap weight imbalance embedded in value-weighted winner-versus-loser portfolios, while the realised return consequence remains noisy.
